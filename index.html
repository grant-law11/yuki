Mom AI

Mom AI is a private, family-run home assistant: a touchscreen in one home that shows the time, the weather and the family calendar, and answers spoken questions.

It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone outside the family. There are no accounts, subscriptions or advertising.

It uses the Google Calendar API to show the family's own calendar events on the screen and, when the person at the screen confirms, to add an event to that calendar.
